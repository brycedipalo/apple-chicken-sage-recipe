Pan-Rushed Chicken with Apples, Onions and Sage

A recreated one-pan chicken recipe with browned chicken, apples, onions, fresh sage, apple cider, chicken broth, grainy mustard, and a touch of apple cider vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs or 4 large chicken breasts
  • 2 apples, cored and cut into wedges
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 8 to 12 fresh sage leaves, chopped
  • 1 cup apple cider
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons grainy mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar, plus more to taste
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons butter or cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt, or to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, or to taste

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken: Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with salt and black pepper.
  2. Brown the chicken: Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the butter or oil, then brown the chicken well on both sides. Transfer the chicken to a plate.
  3. Cook the apples and onions: Add the sliced onion and apple wedges to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion softens and the apples begin to brown.
  4. Add sage and deglaze: Stir in the sage. Pour in the apple cider and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
  5. Build the sauce: Add the chicken broth, grainy mustard and 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar. Stir until combined.
  6. Finish the chicken: Return the chicken and any juices to the skillet. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has reduced slightly, about 10 to 15 minutes depending on thickness.
  7. Adjust and serve: Taste the sauce and add more apple cider vinegar, salt or pepper if needed. Spoon the apples, onions and sauce over the chicken and serve.
